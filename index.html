Why Rootwork

We got tired of gear that looked good in a photo and fell apart in six weeks.

Rootwork started in 2021 with one question: what does fitness equipment look like if you design it to last a decade, not a season? The answer was fewer synthetics, more natural rubber and cork, and a supply chain we could actually trace.

Every band is stress-tested to 20,000 stretches. Every mat is cut from cork harvested without felling a single tree.
